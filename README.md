# Urban Fishing

A Florida fishing-and-sneaking game that runs in the browser. You start in Port Mullet with a garage-sale rod and $30. Every pond, canal, storm drain and fancy reflecting pool in town has fish in it, and half of them say NO FISHING.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is in that one file. Your progress saves in the browser.

- Arrow keys / WASD or tap to walk
- **F** or Space to cast, hook and reel
- **E** to talk or ride the bus
- **B** to switch bait, **K** to launch a kayak or skiff
- **M** for the map, **J** for the journal

## What's in it

- A whole town: neighborhoods, a golf course, an office park, a power station, a theme park, a gated community, an airport, downtown, the bay and offshore
- Guards, cameras and patrols to sneak past
- 17 fishing spots and 20 legends to catch
- Two story acts: saving Lou's Bait & Tackle, then taking on TV angler Trent Marlowe
- Weather, a weekly tournament, a bus line, a home aquarium and achievements
